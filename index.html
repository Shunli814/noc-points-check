Not sure of your NOC?

  1. Select “Copy prompt” and paste it into the AI you already use.
  2. Replace the last line of the prompt with your job description, then send it.
  3. Paste the last line of the AI’s answer (NOC codes: …) into the box at the top and select “Check”.

The prompt tells the AI to go by job duties, not the title, and not to lean toward a NOC that earns points. Different AIs may give different answers. Treat them all as a starting point.

High-wage list and category-based draws

Points and category draws are separate. An occupation can earn no high-wage points and still belong to a category with its own invitation rounds. The French-language category does not depend on occupation, so it is not listed here.

The rules: at least 12 months of eligible Canadian work experience in a listed occupation adds 70, 40 or 20 points depending on the tier. A qualifying job offer in a listed occupation is worth 100, 70 or 50 points. The two are not added together: you get the higher one. In effect from November 3, 2026.

Category-based selection: IRCC holds separate invitation rounds for specific categories. Being in a category only makes you eligible for those rounds. Whether you are invited still depends on the cut-off score and requirements of each round.

The high-wage list comes from the IRCC high-wage occupations page and the category lists from the IRCC category-based selection page. Both pages were last modified on 2026-10-16. The lists can change, so check the official pages.

This page is a lookup aid, not immigration advice. IRCC decides your NOC from your actual job duties, and the same job title can fall under different NOCs. For your own case, talk to a licensed immigration consultant or lawyer.
